Everything you need to protect a position, in plain English.

Quick start
  1. Connect your wallet.
  2. Open Protect and choose the token you want to protect.
  3. Pick an amount, then a strategy: Meme coin rocket, Conservative lock or Risk-free moonbag.
  4. Check the estimate, then press Activate auto-protection and sign once.
  5. Watch it under My positions. You can cancel any time.
A worked example

You protect 1,000 tokens bought at $1.00 with a +15% start gain and an 8% step-1 buffer. The price climbs to a peak of $2.00, so your stop sits at $1.84. If it falls there, step 1 sells 40% (400 tokens) for about $736 before the 0.30% swap fee. The peak then resets to that exit price, and step 2 trails at 6%. If the price rallies instead, the peak ratchets up again and nothing more is sold.

Questions

How does the stop price work?

Once your token is up by your start gain, PulseTrail tracks the highest price reached. Your stop sits a set percentage below that peak. Within each step the stop only moves up; after a step sells, the peak resets to the sale price and the next step trails from there.

What are the three exit steps?

Step 1 sells 40% of your position when price falls 8% (default) from the peak. The peak then resets to that exit price and step 2 trails from there at 6% to sell 30%, then step 3 trails at 5% and sells the last 30%. You can set each step's buffer.

What is smart wiggle room?

PulseTrail looks at the last 8 price ticks and averages the size of the moves, ignoring tiny 0.05% noise. When swings are large it adds extra room to your buffer, up to a cap (10% by default). When the market is calm the extra room is about zero.

Do you hold my keys or tokens?

No. PulseTrail never holds your private keys. When you activate protection, your tokens are safely held in the verified, non-custodial Base vault contract, and you can cancel and withdraw them back to your wallet at any time from My positions.

Which strategy should I pick?

Meme coin rocket starts at +15% with a wide 12% buffer for volatile coins. Conservative lock starts at +8% with a tight 5% buffer to bank gains early. Risk-free moonbag starts at +30% with an 8% buffer, aimed at getting your original money out while the rest rides. Pro mode lets you set your own numbers.

What does the safety check look at?

It checks that you can sell the token (no honeypot trap), that your trade is a tiny share of the pool so it will not move the price, and that there is no hidden transfer tax on exit. A token that fails cannot be protected.

How do I change or cancel a protection?

Open My positions. Tighten or Loosen moves your safety buffer by 1%. Cancel and withdraw stops the protection and leaves any unsold tokens in your wallet.

What does it cost?

PulseTrail charges no fee. When a step sells, the exchange pool takes its standard 0.30% swap fee, and you pay Base gas, typically $0.005 to $0.015 per transaction. Reactive callback gas is covered by the contract.

Where is the proof it works?

The Proof page lists the contract, latency and transactions so anyone can verify them on Base.

What are the limits?

Safety buffers must be between 0.5% and 50%, and slippage is capped at 20%. The app warns you if your order is more than 5% of the pool's reserves, because it could move the price.

What are the risks?

Swaps can slip in fast markets, and no tool can guarantee a sale price. This page uses simulated prices for demonstration and is not financial advice.
